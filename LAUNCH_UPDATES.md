## LinkedIn Post

```text
I just deployed LEMMA, a public AI research assistant that is built around one principle: no claim without evidence.

Most RAG demos stop at "the model answered." LEMMA adds the next layer: can the system prove where the answer came from, and can it check whether retrieval is still working?

What it does:
- Upload documents and ask questions
- Uses hybrid retrieval with dense + sparse search
- Sends retrieved chunks to Claude with citation-enabled document blocks
- Returns answers with source-backed citations
- Includes a live reliability panel with recall probes, canary checks, latency, and cost tracking
- Runs as a deployable FastAPI app on Railway with Qdrant local mode

Live demo:
https://lemma-production-b84f.up.railway.app

This is part of my AI engineering portfolio while I continue building toward production-grade AI systems: retrieval, evaluation, reliability, observability, and practical deployment.
```
